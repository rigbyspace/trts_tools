\section{Introduction: The Rejection of Undefined Operations}
%=====================================================================

In standard continuous mathematics, division by zero is undefined. It produces infinities and singularities that break the equations of General Relativity and Quantum Field Theory, requiring ad-hoc renormalization or the postulation of inaccessible event horizons. Empty space is treated as a continuous vacuum with a zero-point energy that suffers from a $10^{120}$ order-of-magnitude cosmological constant problem.

In RigbySpace (RS), zero is not a number; it is absolute absence. Because the framework strictly forbids division as an ontological primitive, a zero in the denominator of an Explicit Rational Pair (ERP) does not trigger a mathematical infinity. It triggers a deterministic, mechanical routing of structural information.

This document provides the complete, granular formalization of Zero Logic. It demonstrates exactly how the universe handles empty lattice positions ($0/d$) and constraint vacuums ($n/0$), how the Transformative Reciprocal ($\psiop$) resolves singularities without information loss, and how the Cycle Boundary Operator ($\Omega$) processes these states to generate the arrow of time and Residual Causal Depth (Dark Matter).

\section{The Ontology of Zero and the Empty Lattice Position}
%=====================================================================

In RigbySpace, a physical state is an Explicit Rational Pair $(n,d)$. The numerator $n$ encodes kinematic event magnitude. The denominator $d$ encodes geometric capacity (causal history).

\begin{definition}[The Empty Lattice Position]\label{def:empty_lattice}
\begin{rsbox}
The state $0/d$ (where $d \neq 0$) represents an empty lattice position. 

\textbf{1. Kinematic Absence:}
The numerator is absent ($n=0$). There is no kinematic magnitude, no momentum, and no event taking place. An incoming active state that encounters this position occupies it unchanged, because there is nothing present to interact with, resist, or alter it.

\textbf{2. Contextual Propagation Labels:}
In a mediant propagation context, this emptiness is labeled $0/0$. In a multiplicative context, it is labeled $0/1$. Because GCD reduction is forbidden, these labels are never collapsed into each other. They are the exact same absence encountered from different operational angles.

\textbf{3. Preservation of Causal Depth:}
While the position is kinematically empty, the denominator $d$ carries genuine structural history. The position is empty in the physical sense, but non-trivial in the causal sense. The information encoded in $d$ is not lost; it is processed by the $\Omega$ operator.
\end{rsbox}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The Constraint Vacuum]\label{def:constraint_vacuum}
\begin{rsbox}
The state $n/0$ (where $n \neq 0$) is a Constraint Vacuum.

\textbf{1. Loss of Metric Basis:}

\textbf{2. Preservation of History:}
The numerator $n$ preserves the exact structural history of the state prior to the loss of its metric basis. The information is frozen, not destroyed.

\textbf{3. Projective Suspension:}
If any component of a 3D elliptic state $(X, Y, Z)$ becomes $0$, the entire system enters Projective Suspension. The system suspends to the Projective Identity $\IP = (0, 1, 0)$. Evolution is absolutely frozen until the suspension is resolved by a coupled interaction.
\end{rsbox}
\end{definition}

\section{Suspension Resolution via the Transformative Reciprocal}
%=====================================================================

A suspended state ($n/0$) cannot remain frozen forever if it encounters an active state. The Transformative Reciprocal ($\psiop$) is the exact mechanical operator that resolves singularities.

\begin{theorem}[Zero-Logic Resolution]\label{thm:zero_resolution}
\begin{rsbox}
Let a suspended state $n/0$ be paired with an active state $c/d$ (where $c, d \neq 0$).
The Transformative Reciprocal $\psiop$ acts on the coupled pair:
\[ \psiop\left(\frac{n}{0},\; \frac{c}{d}\right) = \left(\frac{d}{n},\; \frac{0}{c}\right) \]

This operation executes a perfect, deterministic routing of structural information:

\textbf{1. The Resolved Active State ($d/n$):}
The suspended history $n$ crosses over to become the new denominator (the new metric basis). The active partner's denominator $d$ crosses over to become the new kinematic magnitude. The first output $d/n$ is fully resolved, active, and returns to standard lattice evolution.

\textbf{2. The Empty Magnitude Trace ($0/c$):}
The active partner's magnitude $c$ crosses over to become the denominator of the second state. Because the suspended state had no metric basis ($0$), the new numerator is $0$. The second output is $0/c$. 

This is an Empty Lattice Position (Definition~\ref{def:empty_lattice}). It has been stripped of its kinematic presence, but it carries the magnitude $c$ as its new causal depth.
\end{rsbox}
\end{theorem}

\begin{observebox}
\textbf{The Conservation of Information:}
In continuous physics, information falling into a singularity is paradoxically lost (the Black Hole Information Paradox). In RigbySpace, the singularity ($n/0$) simply steals the metric basis ($d$) from a passing active state to resurrect itself, while converting the passing state's momentum ($c$) into pure causal depth ($0/c$). No information is lost; it is merely redistributed between the kinematic and structural ledgers.
\end{observebox}
