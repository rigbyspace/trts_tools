## The Transformative Reciprical Operator (ψ)

\[
\psi\left(\frac{a}{b}, \frac{c}{d}\right) = \left(\frac{d}{a}, \frac{b}{c}\right)
\]

In ERP notation:

\[
\psi((a,b), (c,d)) = ((d,a), (b,c))
\]

**Properties:**
- \(\psi^2 =\) full pair exchange (charge conjugation)
- \(\psi^4 =\) identity
- \(\psi\) cannot act on a single ERP — it always acts on a *pair*

## The Aggregate Transform (η / eta)

\[
\eta(n, d) = (n + d, n)
\]

In ERP notation:

\[
\eta((a,b)) = (a + b, a)
\]

**Physical role:** Propagates magnitude while shifting the geometric basis. Applied repeatedly, it generates the Lucas sequence, whose ratios converge to the golden ratio \(\phi\).

---

## The Linear Transform (λ / lambda)

\[
\lambda(n, d) = (n + d, d)
\]

In ERP notation:

\[
\lambda((a,b)) = (a + b, b)
\]

**Physical role:** Propagates magnitude while preserving capacity. Vacuum propagation — the state moves through the lattice without changing its metric basis.

## Fractional Addition (⊕)

\[
(n_1, d_1) \oplus (n_2, d_2) = (n_1 d_2 + n_2 d_1,\; d_1 d_2)
\]

**Null state:** \((0,1)\) — fractional absence.

**Physical role:** Combines states via fractional (exponential) growth. The engine of mass generation — mass is the structural rank of the accumulated ⊕-history.

---

## Mediant Addition (⊞)

\[
(n_1, d_1) \boxplus (n_2, d_2) = (n_1 + n_2,\; d_1 + d_2)
\]

**Null state:** \((0,0)\) — mediant absence.

**Physical role:** Combines states via linear (additive) growth. Used for coordinate accumulation and ledgers in the lower triad.
